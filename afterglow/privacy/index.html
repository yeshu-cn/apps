关于晚安

晚安（Afterglow）由 Yeshu 提供，仅供年满18岁的成年人使用。AI 恋人与约会是虚构体验，不是真实人物、心理治疗或专业服务。生成内容可能错误或不适宜，请自行判断重要信息。请勿用于色情、未成年人亲密互动、骚扰、侵权或违法行为。你保留自己提交内容的权利，并应有权使用该内容。

我们处理哪些信息

应用会创建匿名会话，不要求邮箱、手机号或真实姓名。服务器保存随机会话标识的验证摘要、你填写的称呼与角色设定、聊天及回复、主动保存的记忆、收藏状态、反馈原因与对应回复，以及提供额度和安全控制所需的时间与计数。会话凭据保存在设备钥匙串中，应用缓存使用系统文件保护并排除云备份；这些措施不等同于端到端加密。换机或丢失凭据后，我们无法自动恢复原会话。

AI、网络与第三方

在你明确授权后，聊天正文、近期相关对话、称呼与角色设定及已保存记忆，会经我们位于新加坡的服务器发送至 DeepSeek，用于生成回复。关闭设置中的 AI 数据授权后，不再发起新的 AI 请求。DeepSeek 依其适用的隐私政策和开放平台服务条款处理已收到的数据，可能在中国处理和保留信息。我们未取得针对本服务的零保留或不用于训练的保证，无法承诺第三方的即时删除。请勿发送密码、证件号码、私密健康资料或未经同意的他人信息。信息可能跨境处理。

服务器会临时处理连接 IP；应用服务只将其转换成用于限流的不可直接识别摘要，保留最多48小时。应用的代理访问日志关闭。基础网络和托管服务仍可能为安全运行处理连接信息。应用不请求通讯录、照片、麦克风或定位权限，没有广告、广告跟踪或第三方分析 SDK。

保存、导出与删除

会话数据会保留到你在应用中删除会话；失败的消息可能暂存以支持重试。你可以删除记忆，后续请求不再传入该条记忆，但相关内容可能仍存在于近期对话中。导出的文件由你负责保管与分享。卸载应用不会自动删除服务器记录，钥匙串凭据也可能保留。

你可以在「我们 → 设置 → 删除会话与全部数据」删除服务器会话、聊天、记忆、收藏、反馈以及本机缓存与凭据。在线数据库立即删除；隔离的加密备份最多7天内轮换清除，仅用于灾难恢复。恢复备份时我们会应用删除记录，以避免恢复已删除的会话。你提供给第三方或自行导出的副本无法由我们自动删除。凭据丢失后，可通过下方邮箱联系；我们可能需要不包含敏感正文的必要信息核实请求。

服务与联系

当前版本免费，无订阅或自动扣款。服务可能因维护、容量或其他原因中断；重大条款变化会在本页更新，并在需要时重新征得授权。应用中的不当回复可长按反馈。安全保护不能保证拦截每一种不适宜内容。支持邮件仅用于处理请求，不出售你的内容，不将其用于广告。相关条款不限制你的法定权利。如需支持、查询、更正或删除信息，联系 guan.yeshu@gmail.com。

Privacy Policy and Terms

Afterglow is provided by Yeshu for adults aged 18+. The AI companion and dates are fictional, may be inaccurate and do not replace real relationships, professional advice or emergency services. Do not use the service for explicit sexual content, intimate interactions involving minors, harassment, infringement or illegal activity. You retain rights to your content and must have permission to use it.

Data and AI processing

No email, phone number or real name is required. We store an anonymous session identifier and credential hash, chosen names and character settings, messages and replies, saved memories, favorites, feedback with the relevant reply, timestamps and usage counts. Credentials stay in the device Keychain. Local caches use iOS file protection and are excluded from cloud backup; this is not end-to-end encryption. Device transfer or lost credentials cannot automatically restore a session.

With your explicit permission, messages, recent conversation, character settings and memories are sent through our Singapore server to DeepSeek to generate replies. You may revoke permission in Settings to stop new AI requests. DeepSeek handles received data under its privacy policy and applicable Open Platform terms, and may process and retain data in China. We have no service-specific guarantee of zero retention or exclusion from training and cannot promise immediate provider deletion. Do not send credentials, government identifiers, private health information or others' data without permission. Processing may cross borders.

Connection IPs are processed transiently and converted to a rate-limiting digest retained for no more than 48 hours by our application. Proxy access logs are disabled; hosting and network infrastructure may process connection information for security. No advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDK, contact, photo, microphone or location permission is used.

Retention and your choices

Records remain until you delete your session. Failed messages may be retained for retries. Deleting a memory removes it from future memory context, although recent messages may contain related information. You control exported files. Uninstalling does not delete server data and may retain Keychain credentials.

Use Together → Settings → Delete session and all data to remove your server account, conversations, memories, favorites and feedback, plus local cache and credentials. Live records are deleted immediately. Encrypted disaster-recovery backups rotate within seven days. Deletion markers are applied after restoring a backup so deleted sessions are not restored. Copies you exported or shared and data already received by third parties cannot be deleted automatically. Contact us for data requests; reasonable verification may be required if credentials are lost.

Service and contact

The initial version is free with no subscriptions or automatic charges. Availability may be interrupted. Material policy changes will be reflected here and permission renewed when required. Long-press an AI reply to report inappropriate content; safeguards cannot prevent every unsuitable reply. Support email is used to address requests. We do not sell your content or use it for advertising. These terms do not limit statutory rights. Contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com.
