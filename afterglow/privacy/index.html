About Afterglow

Afterglow is provided by Yeshu for adults aged 18+. The AI companion and dates are fictional, may make mistakes and cannot replace real relationships, professional advice or emergency services. Do not use the service for explicit sexual content, intimate interactions involving minors, harassment, infringement or illegal activity. You retain rights to your content and must have permission to submit it.

Your account and information

You sign in with Apple or Google through Firebase Authentication. Firebase may receive your provider identifier, name and email address, including an Apple private relay address if selected. We do not receive your Apple or Google password. Your Firebase user identifier connects your companion settings, conversations, saved memories, favorite replies, reports and usage counts to your account, so signing in with the same account can restore them on another device. Different sign-in methods may create separate accounts; we do not automatically merge them.

Our Firebase project uses Cloud Firestore in the United States and Cloud Functions in us-central1. Conversation content, names, companion settings and memories are encrypted by our application before Firestore storage. Keys remain server-side in Secret Manager. Local copies use iOS file protection and are excluded from device cloud backup. Authentication credentials are managed by Firebase's secure storage. This is not end-to-end encryption; our service must decrypt relevant content to generate replies and provide exports.

AI processing and third parties

Only after your separate affirmative consent, your message, recent conversation, companion settings and saved memories are sent through Firebase to DeepSeek to generate a reply. Turn off AI data processing in Settings to stop new requests. DeepSeek may process and retain information in China under its privacy policy and Open Platform terms. We have no service-specific guarantee of zero retention or exclusion from training and cannot promise immediate deletion by that provider. Do not submit passwords, government identifiers, private health information or others' information without consent. Processing may cross borders.

Google/Firebase and Apple process sign-in and service information under their applicable policies. See Firebase privacy information, Google Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy. Cloud infrastructure may process connection IPs and service request metadata for operation and security. Our application does not log chat bodies, model response bodies or authentication tokens. We do not include an advertising or analytics SDK, track users across apps, or request contacts, photos, microphone or location access.

Retention and control

Your records remain until you delete your account or individual memories. Failed requests may remain temporarily to support retries. Removing a memory removes it from future memory context; recent chat history may still mention related information. Exported and shared copies are under your control. Signing out clears this device's content cache but keeps your cloud account. Uninstalling does not delete cloud records.

Use Us → Settings → Delete account to initiate permanent deletion. Sign in again when requested to verify ownership; Apple sign-in authorization is revoked as part of Apple account deletion. The server deletes conversations, memories, saved replies and reports, and removes the Firebase Authentication account. A minimal deletion marker and short-lived quota records may remain to prevent a request already in flight from recreating deleted data or abusing service limits. Managed infrastructure may retain operational or recovery versions under its own retention policies; data already sent to DeepSeek and files you exported cannot be automatically removed by us. Contact us if you cannot access your account or complete deletion.

Service and contact

The initial version is free with no subscriptions or automatic charges. Messages reset at 00:00 UTC. Availability may be interrupted by maintenance or capacity limits. Touch and hold an AI reply to report it; safety measures cannot prevent every unsuitable reply. Material policy changes will be reflected here and consent renewed where required. We do not sell your content or use it for advertising. These terms do not limit your statutory rights. For support, access, correction or deletion requests, contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com.
