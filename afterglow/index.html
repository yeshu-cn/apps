Afterglow

A little closer, every day.

Afterglow is an AI companion for adults. Share your day, imagine a date and keep the little things you want remembered.

  • A companion you shape: name, personality and pronouns.
  • Conversations, imagined dates, memories and saved replies.
  • Apple and Google accounts, with records saved to your account.
  • English and Simplified Chinese.
  • 30 free messages a day. No subscription or advertising.

iPhone and iPad · 18+ · Preparing for release

The companion is AI. Romance and dates are fictional experiences.
