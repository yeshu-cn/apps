Sign in and return

Use the same Apple or Google account on each device. Different login methods may create separate accounts. If authorization was canceled, try again. Never send us your password or full private conversations.

A reply did not arrive

Check your connection, then retry the pending message. Completed replies are reused rather than generated twice; failed generations do not use your personal allowance. The 30-message daily allowance resets at 00:00 UTC.

Your data

Keep memories and saved replies in Us. Settings lets you edit your companion, switch English or Simplified Chinese, export records, revoke AI consent, sign out or delete your account. Deletion may ask you to sign in again. Delete your account before uninstalling if you want cloud records removed.

A reply feels wrong

Touch and hold it and choose Report reply. If you are in immediate danger, contact local emergency services or a trusted person nearby. The AI cannot provide in-person help.

帮助与支持

请使用同一 Apple 或 Google 账号在不同设备登录。不同登录方式可能建立不同账号。网络恢复后可重试未完成消息，失败生成不扣个人额度。你可以在「我们 → 设置」切换语言、管理角色、导出、撤回 AI 授权、退出或删除账号。删除可能要求重新登录核实身份。长按回复可反馈不当内容。若有即时危险，请联系当地紧急服务或身边可信任的人。联系支持时请提供设备、系统版本及问题描述，不要发送密码或完整私人对话。
