巧伴 · MakeBuddy

把你的想法，做成你的应用。

中文 · English

把日常需要告诉巧伴，用 AI 创造自己的小应用。

从一个想法开始，做出可以操作的界面；用起来，再通过对话慢慢调整。小应用在巧伴内运行，记录保存在本机。

内置待办和日记示例可直接体验。AI 创建与修改需使用自己的 DeepSeek API Key，相关费用由 DeepSeek 账户承担。当前不提供云同步，请备份重要资料。

巧伴目前处于发布准备阶段，尚未在 App Store 公开上架。

Your ideas, your own apps.

Describe what you need and use AI to create personal mini apps. Use them inside MakeBuddy and keep shaping them through conversation.

Try the included to-do and journal examples without a key. AI features require your own DeepSeek API key, with usage billed to your DeepSeek account. Apps and records stay on your device. Cloud sync is not included; keep backups of important data.

MakeBuddy is being prepared for release and is not yet publicly available on the App Store. The current app interface is Simplified Chinese.
