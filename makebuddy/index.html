巧伴 · MakeBuddy

把你的想法，做成你的应用。

中文 · English

把日常需要告诉巧伴，用 AI 创造自己的小应用。

从一个想法开始，做出可以操作的界面；用起来，再通过对话慢慢调整。小应用在巧伴内运行，记录和对话保存在本机。

内置待办和日记示例无需登录即可体验。使用 AI 创建或修改时，通过 Apple 或 Google 登录，无需自备 API Key。符合条件的登录身份可领取一次性 AI 体验额度，也可选择 MakeBuddy Pro 自动续订订阅；实际价格和服务额度以应用内购买页为准。

AI 请求中的消息、相关历史和任务文件会经巧伴云端服务发送给 Google Gemini。登录不提供云同步，卸载前请导出重要资料。AI 结果可能出错，请检查实际功能。

巧伴目前处于发布准备阶段，尚未在 App Store 公开上架。

Your ideas, your own apps.

Describe what you need and use AI to create personal mini apps. Use them inside MakeBuddy and keep shaping them through conversation. Apps, records and conversations stay on your device.

Try the included to-do and journal examples without signing in. For AI creation and changes, sign in with Apple or Google; no model API key is required. Eligible sign-in identities receive a one-time AI trial. An optional auto-renewable MakeBuddy Pro subscription provides ongoing AI usage; see the in-app purchase screen for prices and allowances.

AI requests send your messages, relevant history and task files through MakeBuddy's cloud service to Google Gemini. Sign-in does not synchronize your files. Export backups before uninstalling, and check AI-generated results.

MakeBuddy is being prepared for release and is not yet publicly available on the App Store. The main interface is Simplified Chinese; account and subscription screens also include English text.
