Create your own apps with AI.

中文 · English

MakeBuddy is an AI app builder for personal mini apps. Describe what you need, create a tool without coding, and keep shaping it through conversation.

Create tools for your everyday life

Build a to-do list, a journal, a reading log or another small personal tool. Use the interface, save your records, then ask for supported changes when your needs evolve. Mini apps run inside MakeBuddy; they are not separate installed apps or APKs.

Keep your records with you

Your mini apps, records and conversations are stored on your device. Export JSON backups and import them later. On Android, add a Home Screen shortcut or choose a mini app to open when MakeBuddy launches. Signing in does not provide cloud sync.

Try an example, then create with AI

The included To-do and Mood Journal examples work without signing in. AI creation and changes require an internet connection, Google or Apple sign-in, and available AI credits. No model API key is needed. Eligible identities receive a one-time AI trial; optional MakeBuddy Pro subscriptions provide ongoing usage. See the purchase screen for the actual price and allowance.

AI requests send messages, relevant history and task files through MakeBuddy’s cloud service to Google Gemini. Check generated results and export important records before uninstalling.

MakeBuddy for Android and iOS is being prepared for release. It is not yet publicly available on Google Play or the App Store.
