开始使用

从首页示例入口添加「轻待办」或「心情日记」，无需登录即可体验本机记录功能。需要 AI 创建、对话或修改时，在「设置」中使用 Apple 或 Google 登录；无需填写模型 API Key。

AI 操作会把消息、相关历史和任务所需的小应用文件发送给 Google Gemini。请阅读隐私政策与用户协议，避免发送不必要的敏感资料。AI 结果可能出错，请检查生成页面是否满足需求。

订阅与 AI 额度

符合条件的登录身份可领取一次性 200 AI 体验额度。MakeBuddy Pro 提供月付和年付自动续订订阅，两档均含每月 3,000 AI 额度，年付逐月补充、不结转；实际价格以购买页面为准。在「设置 → 账号详情」可查看余额和最近任务消耗。额度用尽后暂停新的 AI 操作，不自动收取超额费用；已有小应用的普通本机使用、保存和导出不受影响。

恢复购买时，请登录原巧伴账号，在 MakeBuddy Pro 页面选择「恢复购买」。管理或取消订阅，请前往 Apple 订阅设置。恢复不会重发已使用的额度。扣款后权益暂未到账时，请保留 Apple 交易记录，尝试恢复或联系支持；不要连续重复购买。退款由 Apple 按其流程处理。

保存、恢复与账号

小应用、记录和对话保存在本机，当前没有文件云同步。在小应用菜单导出备份，在设置导入备份；移除的应用可从「设置 → 最近删除」恢复，满 30 天自动清理，也可确认后永久删除。恢复上一版界面会保留当前数据记录。卸载前请导出重要资料。

在设置打开已登录账号详情，可退出登录或验证身份后删除云端账号。这些操作不会删除本机小应用，也不会自动取消 Apple 订阅。为交易核对、退款处理和防止重复领取，必要的交易、用量结算和防重放记录会按隐私政策保留。

连接失败与能力限制

登录失效时请重新登录；AI 请求失败时可重试或修改需求。AI 生成或修改未完成时，本轮暂存修改不会覆盖原应用。长任务请保持应用在前台。AI 额度、任务预算或服务限额用尽时，请查看界面提示及额度详情。

小应用在巧伴内部运行，当前不支持独立安装包、文件云同步、系统提醒、后台定时执行、相机或麦克风接入，以及连接外部服务。创建前会评估需求，并对超出能力的部分说明可选方向。

联系支持与报告问题

使用问题、不合适的生成内容、订阅问题或隐私请求，请联系 guan.yeshu@gmail.com。请提供设备型号、系统版本、巧伴版本、操作步骤和已遮去私人资料的截图。不要发送密码、支付凭据、登录令牌或完整私人记录。

Getting started

Add the bundled To-do or Mood journal from the home-screen examples entry to try local records without signing in. Sign in with Apple or Google in Settings for AI creation, chat and changes. No model API key is required.

AI actions send messages, relevant history and files needed for the task to Google Gemini through MakeBuddy's cloud service. Read the Privacy Policy and Terms, avoid unnecessary sensitive information and check generated results.

Subscriptions and AI usage

Eligible sign-in identities receive a one-time 200-credit trial. Monthly and annual auto-renewable MakeBuddy Pro plans both include 3,000 AI credits per month; annual credits replenish monthly without rollover. Prices are shown before purchase. View balance and recent task usage in Settings → account details. When credits run out, new AI work pauses with no automatic overage charge. Ordinary local use, saving and export remain available.

To restore, sign in to the original MakeBuddy account and select Restore purchases on the Pro screen. Manage or cancel your subscription in Apple subscription settings. Restore does not duplicate used credits. If a payment has not delivered access, keep the Apple transaction record and try restore or contact support; avoid repeated purchases. Apple handles refund requests.

Data, recovery and accounts

Apps, records and conversations remain on your device; file cloud sync is not included. Export backups from the mini-app menu and import them in Settings. Recently Deleted items can be recovered for 30 days or permanently deleted after confirmation. Restoring the previous interface preserves current records. Export important data before uninstalling.

Open account details in Settings to sign out or verify and delete the cloud sign-in account. Neither action deletes local files or cancels an Apple subscription. Necessary purchase, settlement and anti-replay records are retained as described in the privacy policy.

Connection and capability limits

Sign in again if your session expires. Retry failed AI work or revise the request. Unfinished staged AI changes do not overwrite the original app. Keep the app open for long-running tasks. Check on-screen notices for credit, task-budget or service limits.

Mini apps run inside MakeBuddy. Standalone installers, file cloud sync, system reminders, scheduled background work, camera/microphone access and external service integrations are not supported. Requirements are assessed before creation.

Support and reports

For help, inappropriate generated content, subscription issues or privacy requests, email guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Include device and OS, app version, steps and redacted screenshots. Never send passwords, payment credentials, sign-in tokens or complete private records.
