更新日期：2026 年 10 月 22 日 · Updated October 22, 2026

关于巧伴

本说明适用于 Yeshu 提供的巧伴（MakeBuddy）。你保留自己内容的权利，请仅处理有权使用的内容，不得用于侵害他人权益或违法用途。AI 生成结果可能不准确、不可用或不适合你的用途，请核对重要信息并保存备份。

本机资料

你的小应用、记录、对话和设置保存在设备上。巧伴目前不提供账户或云同步，也没有由开发者运营的资料存储服务器。API Key 保存在设备钥匙串中，用于向 DeepSeek 验证请求，不会写入小应用文件或导出备份。

AI 与第三方处理

使用 AI 创建、对话或修改时，消息、相关对话及当前任务所需的小应用文件（可能包括你保存的记录）会从设备发送给 DeepSeek，以完成生成或修改。测试连接也会向 DeepSeek 发送测试请求。DeepSeek 会收到 API 身份验证信息及处理连接所需的网络信息。离线使用内置示例和一般本地页面操作不需要发送 AI 请求；页面中需要 AI 的操作仍会连接 DeepSeek。

DeepSeek 按其隐私政策及与你的服务约定处理资料，资料可能在中国或你所在国家或地区以外处理；我们不能承诺第三方不保留或不使用已发送的资料。请勿发送不必要的敏感信息，也不要提交没有授权处理的他人资料。

保留、移除与备份

本机资料会保留到你删除或卸载应用。移除的小应用及其记录会进入「最近删除」，仍保留在设备上并可恢复。卸载巧伴会移除其本机应用资料；系统钥匙串中的密钥可能继续保留，建议先在设置中移除。你导出到其他位置的备份不会随卸载删除，由你自行管理。

你可以停止使用 AI，并在设置中移除 API Key，以停止后续 AI 连接；这不会删除已发送给 DeepSeek 的资料。卸载或删除本机内容也不会删除第三方已保存的副本。如需处理第三方资料，请联系该服务商。

费用与跟踪

巧伴当前没有内购或订阅。AI API 服务由你与 DeepSeek 单独约定并承担相应费用。巧伴没有广告或广告跟踪，也未集成使用分析 SDK。

联系与更新

如需支持或请求查询、更正、删除我们通过支持邮件收到的个人信息，请联系 guan.yeshu@gmail.com。支持邮件仅用于处理请求；请勿发送 API Key、密码或不必要的私人资料。本说明发生重要变化时会更新此页，不限制你依法享有的权利。

Privacy Policy and Terms

This notice applies to MakeBuddy (巧伴), provided by Yeshu. You retain rights to your content and must have permission to use it. Do not use the app to violate others’ rights or applicable law. AI output may be inaccurate, unusable, or unsuitable. Check important results and keep backups.

On-device information

Mini apps, records, conversations, and settings are stored on your device. MakeBuddy currently has no accounts, cloud sync, or developer-operated content storage server. Your API key is stored in the device Keychain, authenticates DeepSeek requests, and is not included in mini-app files or exported backups.

AI and third parties

AI creation, chat, and changes send your messages, relevant conversation history, and mini-app files needed for the task, which may include saved records, directly to DeepSeek. Connection tests also send a test request. DeepSeek receives API authentication and network information needed to handle the connection. Offline examples and ordinary local page actions do not require AI requests; AI-powered page actions still connect to DeepSeek.

DeepSeek processes information under its Privacy Policy and your service agreement. Data may be processed in China or outside your country or region. We cannot promise that the provider does not retain or use transmitted data. Avoid unnecessary sensitive information and other people’s information that you are not authorized to process.

Retention, removal, and backups

Local content remains until you delete it or uninstall the app. Removed mini apps and their records stay in Recently Deleted and can be restored. Uninstalling removes the app’s local content; Keychain credentials may remain, so remove the key in Settings first. Exported backups are separate copies that you manage.

Stop using AI and remove the API key in Settings to stop future AI connections. This does not delete data already sent to DeepSeek. Deleting local content or uninstalling does not delete copies held by third parties. Contact the provider for requests about those copies.

Fees and tracking

MakeBuddy currently has no in-app purchases or subscriptions. You arrange and pay for DeepSeek API use separately. MakeBuddy has no advertising, advertising tracking, or integrated usage analytics SDK.

Contact and updates

For support or requests about personal information you sent to us by email, contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Support emails are used to handle your request. Do not send API keys, passwords, or unnecessary private information. We update this notice for material changes. It does not limit your statutory rights.
