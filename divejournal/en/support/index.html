Help and support

Subscribe and restore

Open Profile → DiveJournal Plus, choose a monthly or yearly plan, sign in and purchase through Apple. After changing devices, use the same app account and Apple purchasing account, then tap Restore purchases. Restoring a subscription does not restore local journals.

Prices are unavailable

Plans are configured for all current App Store territories. If prices have not loaded, check your connection and tap Reload prices. Storefront configuration changes may take time to appear. Manual journaling remains available.

No credits or benefits after purchase

Check that you are signed in to the account used to purchase, check your connection and restore purchases. Pending approval, temporary Apple verification issues, expiry or refunds can affect AI access. Do not buy again or delete and recreate your account. The paywall shows remaining credits verified by the server.

AI failures and retries

Failed requests do not use credits. A retry shortly after a connection interruption can retrieve a completed result without another deduction. After the cache window, a new request is needed. Use up to 8 photos per request and split long conversations. Original journal content can still be edited and saved manually.

Cancel a subscription or delete an account

Manage renewal in your Apple Account subscription settings. Deleting the app or your account does not cancel the subscription. Manage active subscriptions before deleting your account in Profile → Settings → Account management. Contact support to request deletion of associated server billing information.

Contact us

Email guan.yeshu@gmail.com with the app version, time of the issue and error message. Do not send passwords, full payment details, signed Apple transactions or API credentials.

Replies and illustrations

Plus includes 500 replies and 20 new illustrations per month, with separate balances shown in the app. Annual plans also refill monthly without rollover. New accounts can try 10 replies and 2 illustrations once. Failed requests and viewing existing illustrations do not use credits. Running out of illustrations leaves remaining replies available.
