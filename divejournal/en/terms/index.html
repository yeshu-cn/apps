Subscriptions and terms of use

Plans

Monthly and yearly subscriptions provide the same Plus features. There is no free trial. Price, currency and billing period are shown on the App Store purchase screen for your region. China storefront prices are ¥48/month and ¥398/year.

Included credits

Each credit month includes 50 successful AI replies. Each successful reply and/or draft uses one credit, including further conversation to refine a journal. Failed requests do not use credits. Retrieving an already completed result by retrying the same request within the cache window does not use another credit.

Credits refill monthly on the day corresponding to your original purchase date, or the last day of shorter months. Yearly plans refill monthly while active. Unused credits do not roll over. Each request supports up to 8 photos, with a limit of 20 attempts per day and one request processing at a time per account. Split overly long content into separate records. Service may pause at safety capacity limits or when a provider is unavailable. Unsuccessful requests do not use monthly credits.

Purchase, renewal and restore

The subscription is linked to the app account signed in at purchase. Restore with that same app account and the original Apple purchasing account. Apple handles payment and automatic renewal. Manage or cancel renewal in your Apple Account subscription settings. After cancellation, access generally continues until expiry. Refund, revocation or expiry ends AI benefits.

Deleting the app or your sign-in account does not cancel your Apple subscription. Restoring purchases restores benefits, not local journals. A subscription does not provide cloud journal backup or automatically grant permission to send content to AI.

Content and service

AI drafts and species identification may be inaccurate. Review them before saving. Do not use them to assess fitness to dive, create decompression plans or replace on-site professional guidance. Dive learning and refresher materials do not replace formal training.

Free features let you view, edit and manually save local journals. Keep separate copies of important records. Device loss, app deletion or local storage damage can cause data loss.
