Subscriptions and terms of use

Subscriptions and credits

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically, offer the same benefits and have no free trial. Apple confirms price and currency before purchase. Each subscription month includes 50 successful AI replies, including follow-up replies. Failed requests and short-term retrieval of the same completed result do not consume another credit. Credits refill on the original purchase day each month, or the last day of shorter months, without rollover.

Usage limits

Each request supports up to 8 photos, with up to 20 requests daily and one active request per account. Split overly long content. Capacity, network or provider issues may delay service. Purchasing does not automatically authorize content sharing.

Renewal, restore and refunds

Manage or cancel renewal in Apple subscription settings. Access generally continues until expiry after cancellation; refund, revocation or expiry ends paid access. Restore with the original Apple purchase account and app sign-in account. Deleting the app or account does not cancel subscriptions.

Content and storage

Review results before saving. They are for reference, not fitness-to-dive assessment, decompression planning, professional guidance or training. Basic local records remain free. Keep backups; purchase restoration restores access, not records.
