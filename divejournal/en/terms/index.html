Subscriptions and terms of use

Subscriptions and credits

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically and offer the same benefits. China prices are ¥78/month or ¥798/year; Apple confirms local price and currency before purchase. Each subscription month includes 500 successful assistant replies and 20 successfully generated new illustrations, counted separately. Chat, photo identification and journal assistance share replies, including follow-ups. Running out of illustrations does not affect remaining replies. Failed requests, retrieval of the same completed result and viewing existing illustrations do not use another credit. Allowances refill on the original purchase day each month, or the last day of shorter months, without rollover. Annual plans also refill monthly.

New accounts without a subscription can try 10 replies and 2 illustrations once, without a purchase or monthly reset. This is not an auto-renewing subscription trial. Existing accounts migrate proportionally to their remaining previous allowance, without receiving the trial again.

Usage limits

Each request supports up to 8 photos, with at most 2 active requests per account. Frequent requests may need to wait; split overly long content. Capacity, network or provider issues may delay service. Failed requests do not use credits. Purchasing does not automatically authorize content sharing.

Renewal, restore and refunds

Manage or cancel renewal in Apple subscription settings. Access generally continues until expiry after cancellation; refund, revocation or expiry ends paid access. Restore with the original Apple purchase account and app sign-in account. Deleting the app or account does not cancel subscriptions.

Content and storage

Review results before saving. They are for reference, not fitness-to-dive assessment, decompression planning, professional guidance or training. Basic local records remain free. Keep backups; purchase restoration restores access, not records.
