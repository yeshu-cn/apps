Privacy policy

Scope

This policy applies to DiveJournal, provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. Contact: guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Updated September 21, 2026.

Information and purposes

Photos, audio, text and profile information are stored locally by default. Sign-in processes Apple-provided identifiers, authorization information, name and email. Purchase and usage records manage subscriptions, credits and service security. We do not receive payment-card details.

Permissions and third parties

We access selected photos only. Manage microphone and other permissions in system settings. Apple may process speech transcription online. After sign-in and explicit consent, relevant text and photos are processed by Google’s AI service; original audio is not sent to that AI service. Withdraw sending permission in Profile → Settings → AI journal assistant; manual records remain available. Apple provides sign-in and payments; Google (Firebase) provides account and online services. Our server is in Singapore, and processing may occur abroad.

Retention

Original AI inputs are not written to our server database. Results are temporarily stored for connection recovery, become unavailable after 15 minutes, and are periodically deleted; outages may delay deletion. Request-verification records remain for up to 90 days and daily rate-limit records for about two days. Subscription links and aggregate credit records are retained as needed to maintain access and prevent repeated claims. Provider security and access logs follow their policies.

Deletion and contact

Account settings let you delete your sign-in account, revoke Apple authorization and clear that account’s local records. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of identifiable support or billing information. Account deletion does not cancel Apple subscriptions or erase Apple purchase records. The app does not provide cloud backup; manage local content, exports and system backups separately.

Other information

The app has no ads or cross-app tracking. Support emails are used to resolve issues; website hosts may keep access logs. We update this policy for material changes and seek consent again when needed. See the Google and Apple privacy policies.
