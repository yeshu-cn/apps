Privacy policy

Scope

This policy applies to DiveJournal (fun.yeshu.DiveJournal), provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. Contact: guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Updated September 18, 2026.

Local records and permissions

Journals, selected photos, audio and profile details are stored on your device. Cloud backup and cross-device journal sync are not currently available. Deleting the app removes local content; system backups and exported copies follow their own settings. Each signed-in account has separate local records.

Choosing photos does not grant access to your entire photo library. Recording uses the microphone. Speech recognition uses on-device processing when supported; otherwise Apple may process it online. Original audio is not sent to Gemini.

Sign-in and AI

Sign in with Apple and Firebase Authentication process account identifiers, credentials, and the name and email Apple provides, which may be a private relay email. These are used for authentication and account display.

Only after you sign in and explicitly permit AI data sending are the relevant journal conversation and selected photos sent through our Firebase server to Google Gemini to generate replies and drafts for your review. Our server is in Singapore; third parties may process data outside your country or region. Withdraw permission in Profile → Settings → AI journal assistant. Manual journaling remains available.

Subscriptions and credits

Apple handles payments, renewals and refunds. We do not receive payment-card details. Our server stores the linked account token, transaction and original transaction identifiers, product, environment, expiry and refund status. It checks subscription status with Apple and records AI credits, request status and token usage to provide benefits, prevent duplicate deductions and limit abnormal usage.

Caching, retention and deletion

We do not write original AI input text, photos or audio to our server database. Generated results are briefly encrypted and cached so completed replies can be retrieved after a connection interruption. They stop being available after 15 minutes and are deleted by a cleanup job that runs every 15 minutes. Cleanup can be delayed during an outage.

Request status and content hashes are retained for up to 90 days. Daily rate-limit records are cleaned up after approximately two days. Subscription links and aggregate credit records are retained as needed to provide the service and prevent a transaction being claimed by multiple accounts. Infrastructure may process security logs including IP addresses, request times and error codes. Google/Firebase and Apple apply their own retention policies.

Account settings let you delete your Firebase sign-in account, revoke Apple authorization and clear that account’s local records. Deleting an account does not cancel its Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions before deletion, and contact support to request cleanup of associated billing records. Apple handles requests concerning the transaction records it retains. Do not delete and recreate your account to restore a subscription.

Your choices and contact

There are no ads or cross-app tracking, and the app does not read advertising identifiers. Information you email to support is used for support and troubleshooting. Do not send passwords, payment credentials or unnecessary personal information. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of associated information. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may generate access logs.
