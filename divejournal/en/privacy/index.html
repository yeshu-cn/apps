Privacy policy

Scope

This policy applies to DiveJournal, provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. Contact: guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Updated September 23, 2026.

Information and purposes

Photos, audio, text and profile information are stored locally by default. Sign-in processes Apple-provided identifiers, authorization information, name and email. Purchase and usage records manage subscriptions, credits and service security. We do not receive payment-card details.

Permissions and third parties

We access selected photos only. Manage microphone and other permissions in system settings. Apple may process speech transcription online. At sign-in, an unchecked agreement control explains AI processing and requires your affirmative consent. When using AI, relevant conversations, selected photos and record content needed for your request pass through Firebase to Google Gemini for replies, identification, record assistance and illustrations. Original audio is not sent to Gemini. Withdraw or restore consent in Profile → About → Privacy policy; manual records remain available. Apple provides sign-in and payments; Google (Firebase) provides account and online services. Our server is in Singapore, and processing may occur abroad.

Retention

Original AI inputs are not written to our server database. Reply results are temporarily cached for recovery; access ends after 15 minutes and scheduled cleanup removes them, with possible delays during failures. Illustrations based on encounter photos are isolated by account and encrypted temporarily, become unavailable after 15 minutes, and are deleted by subsequent cleanup. Your device retains an offline copy. Generic illustrations generated only from species names, without personal photos or records, may be shared and reused on the server. Request verification records are retained for up to 90 days and rate-limit records for about two days. Subscription links and cumulative allowances are retained as needed to maintain entitlements and prevent repeated grants. Third-party security and access logs follow their policies.

Deletion and contact

Account settings let you delete your sign-in account, revoke Apple authorization and clear that account’s local records. Email us to request access, correction or deletion of identifiable support or billing information. Account deletion does not cancel Apple subscriptions or erase Apple purchase records. The app does not provide cloud backup; manage local content, exports and system backups separately.

Other information

The app has no ads or cross-app tracking. Support emails are used to resolve issues; website hosts may keep access logs. We update this policy for material changes and seek consent again when needed. See the Google and Apple privacy policies.
