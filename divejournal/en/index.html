DiveJournal

Keep every blue encounter

Your dive journal

Save dive memories with text, photos and audio. Write and edit journals manually, record marine life you encounter, and explore your personal ocean. Journals stay on your device.

DiveJournal Plus

Let AI turn your conversations and selected photos into a draft for you to review. Monthly and yearly plans each include 50 successful AI replies per monthly period. Failed requests do not use credits, and unused credits do not roll over. Prices and currency are shown by the App Store for your region. The China storefront prices are ¥48/month and ¥398/year.

Available for testing

DiveJournal is currently in TestFlight testing. Public App Store availability and subscription sales require Apple review. Manual journaling does not require a subscription.

Keep your own judgment

AI can make mistakes. Review drafts before saving. Learning and refresher content does not replace formal dive training, on-site professional guidance or medical advice.
