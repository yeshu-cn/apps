DiveJournal

Keep every blue encounter

Your dive journal

Save dive memories with text, photos and audio. Write and edit journals manually, record marine life you encounter, and explore your personal ocean. Journals stay on your device.

DiveJournal Plus

Chat with the assistant, identify marine life in selected photos, and ask it to organize, find or update records. Review saved changes and undo when needed. Monthly and yearly plans each include 500 successful replies and 20 new illustrations per monthly period, counted separately. Failures do not use credits, and unused credits do not roll over. Annual plans also refill monthly. China prices are ¥78/month and ¥798/year; Apple displays your local price and currency.

New accounts can try 10 replies and 2 illustrations once, without purchasing a subscription.

Available for testing

DiveJournal is currently in TestFlight testing. Public App Store availability and subscription sales require Apple review. Manual journaling does not require a subscription.

Keep your own judgment

AI can make mistakes. Check generated results and saved records, and edit or undo when needed. Learning and refresher content does not replace formal dive training, on-site professional guidance or medical advice.
