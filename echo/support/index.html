开始使用

打开回声，按提示使用 Apple 登录。开始聊天前，请阅读隐私政策与用户协议，并决定是否允许 AI 处理任务所需的资料。

在聊天左侧菜单中，你可以新建或切换对话。想留下聊出的计划或笔记时，可以请回声保存，再从「个人空间」查看。

订阅与账户

从左侧菜单进入「账户」，可查看 Echo Pro 方案或恢复购买。订阅可在 Apple 的订阅设置中管理和取消。删除回声账户不会同时取消订阅。

你也可以在「账户」中查看或撤回 AI 资料授权，以及删除回声账户。具体信息请阅读隐私政策与用户协议。

遇到问题

无法登录或发送消息时，请先检查网络，以及「账户」中显示的提示。若仍无法解决，请联系 guan.yeshu@gmail.com。

反馈时可提供应用版本、设备型号、操作步骤及已遮去私人信息的截图。请勿发送密码或完整私人资料。

Getting started

Open Echo and follow the steps to sign in with Apple. Before chatting, read the Privacy Policy and Terms and choose whether AI may process the information needed for your tasks.

Use the sidebar to start or switch conversations. Ask Echo to save a plan or note you want to keep, then find it in your personal space.

Subscriptions and your account

Open Account from the sidebar to view Echo Pro plans or restore purchases. Manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple's subscription settings. Deleting your Echo account does not cancel a subscription.

In Account, you can also review or withdraw AI data-sharing permission and delete your Echo account. See the Privacy Policy and Terms for details.

Need help?

If you cannot sign in or send a message, check your connection and any notice shown in Account. If the problem continues, contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com.

Include the app version, device model, steps to reproduce the problem, and a screenshot with private information removed. Do not send passwords or private documents.
