开始使用

打开小声后可直接浏览初始聊天与「书桌」只读示例。使用 AI 对话前，请按提示使用 Apple 登录并阅读隐私政策与用户协议。

通过聊天页左上角侧边栏可以新建、搜索或切换历史对话。想留下日记、近期安排、旅行手账或你自己的专属主题时，直接在聊天里告诉小声，它会自动整理到「书桌」中；随时可以在「我的 → 小声对你的了解」查看或对话调整小声记住的偏好。

订阅与账户

打开底部「我的 → 账户」，进入「小声 Pro」即可查看按月或按年订阅方案、管理订阅或恢复购买。你也可以随时在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中管理或取消订阅。

在「我的 → 账户」中还可以退出登录或删除小声账户。删除小声账户不会同时取消 Apple 订阅，也不会删除设备本机保存的资料。详情请阅读隐私政策与用户协议。

遇到问题

如果无法登录、无法发送消息或遇到显示异常，请先检查网络连接及「我的 → 账户」中的提示。若问题仍然存在，请联系 guan.yeshu@gmail.com。

联系我们时，欢迎附上应用版本、设备型号、复现步骤及遮去个人隐私的截图。请勿发送密码或完整私人文档。

Getting started

You can explore Noko's opening chat and read-only Desk previews before signing in. To start AI conversations, sign in with Apple and review the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use.

Use the top-left sidebar in Chat to start, search, or switch conversations. Tell Noko naturally about your day, upcoming plans, trips, or any custom topic you want to track — Noko organizes it onto your visual Desk. Review or adjust what Noko remembers anytime under Me → What Noko Knows.

Subscriptions and your account

Open the Me tab, tap Account, and select Noko Pro to view Monthly or Annual plans, manage your subscription, or restore purchases. You can also manage or cancel subscriptions anytime in your Apple Account subscription settings.

Under Me → Account, you can sign out or delete your Noko account. Deleting your Noko account does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase local files on your device. See the Privacy Policy and Terms of Use for details.

Need help?

If you cannot sign in or send a message, please check your internet connection and any notice shown in Me → Account. If the issue persists, contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com.

Please include your app version, device model, steps to reproduce, and screenshots with private information removed. Do not send passwords or private documents.
