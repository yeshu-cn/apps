隐私政策与用户协议

更新日期：2026 年 11 月 25 日 · Updated November 25, 2026

中文 · English

关于本说明

本说明适用于 Yeshu 提供的小声（Noko）。你保留对自己内容的权利，请仅处理你有权使用的内容。AI 回答可能出错，请核对重要信息，并妥善保存重要资料。

账户与付款

使用 Apple 登录时，小声会处理 Apple 账户标识、你选择分享的名称以及登录验证信息，用于创建和验证你的小声账户。小声还会处理订阅交易、订阅状态和服务使用记录，用于提供服务、验证购买和防止滥用。

付款由 Apple 处理，小声不会收到你的银行卡号。小声不含广告，也不进行广告跟踪。

你的内容如何使用

对话记录、书桌保存的文件、核心记忆与偏好设置均保存在你的设备或你选择的本地目录中。登录账户不会自动将这些内容同步到其他设备。

使用小声的 AI 服务时，完成当前任务所需的消息、图片、相关对话上下文、资料与记忆会经由小声服务端发送给第三方 AI 模型服务商，仅用于生成回复、整理书桌内容与延续记忆。在你同意本条款并使用 AI 功能前，应用会征求你的明确同意。

小声的账户记录不包含聊天正文，也不会将聊天内容用于广告。为提供服务和保障安全，小声会保留必要的服务使用、交易和安全记录；网络与错误记录可能包含连接信息。相关第三方服务商按其隐私政策处理传输的数据，数据可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理。请避免发送不必要的高度敏感信息。

当你主动使用语音输入功能时，应用仅在你点击麦克风时请求麦克风或语音识别权限，用于将语音转为文本草稿或消息。

订阅与取消

小声 Pro（Noko Pro）是可选的按月或按年自动续订订阅。价格、订阅周期和权益以确认购买前 Apple 购买页显示的信息为准；除非你在当前周期结束前至少 24 小时取消，否则订阅会自动续订。

你可以在 Apple 账户的订阅设置中随时管理或取消订阅，也可以在应用的「我的 → 账户 → 小声 Pro」中管理订阅或恢复有效购买。服务可用性与额度以应用内实际显示状态为准。

停止使用与删除账户

你可以随时停止发起新的 AI 对话、在「我的 → 账户」退出登录，或删除对话与本机资料。删除某段对话不会自动删除已单独保存到「书桌」里的文档。

你可以从「我的 → 账户」页面删除小声账户。删除后账户会立即停用，服务端账户资料将在 30 天内清除，加密备份最长再保留 30 天。删除账户不会取消 Apple 订阅，也不会删除你设备上的本机文件，需要分别管理。

为防止已删除账户在备份恢复后重新启用并保障服务安全，最少量的伪名化删除记录和安全审计记录可能保留超过上述期限。

联系与更新

Privacy Policy and Terms of Use

This notice applies to Noko (Chinese: 小声), provided by Yeshu. You retain rights to your content and must have permission to use it. AI responses can be inaccurate. Please verify important information and keep copies of important documents.

Your account and payments

When you sign in with Apple, Noko processes your Apple account identifier, the name you choose to share, and sign-in verification information to create and verify your Noko account. Noko also processes subscription transactions, subscription status, and service usage records to provide service, verify purchases, and prevent misuse.

Apple handles payments. Noko does not receive your payment card number. Noko contains no advertising and performs no ad tracking.

How your content is used

Conversations, saved Desk documents, personal memory, and preferences stay on your device or in the local folder you choose. Signing in does not automatically sync this content across devices.

When you use Noko's AI features, the messages, images, relevant conversation history, documents, and memory required for your task pass through Noko's service to a third-party AI model provider solely to generate replies, organize your Desk, and maintain continuity. The app asks for your agreement before you use AI features.

Noko's account records do not store chat bodies, and Noko does not use your content for advertising. Noko retains necessary service usage, transaction, and security logs to operate and protect the service; network and error logs may contain connection metadata. Third-party AI providers process transmitted requests under their own privacy policies and may process data outside your country or region. Please avoid sending unnecessary sensitive personal information.

When you choose to use voice input, Noko requests microphone or speech recognition permission only when you tap voice input, solely to transcribe your speech into chat drafts or messages.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Noko Pro is an optional monthly or annual auto-renewing subscription. Pricing, billing periods, and benefits are shown by Apple before you confirm your purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

You can manage or cancel your subscription anytime in your Apple Account subscription settings, and restore eligible purchases under Me → Account → Noko Pro. Service availability and allowance limits are shown in the app.

Stopping use and account deletion

You can stop sending AI requests at any time, sign out under Me → Account, or delete conversations and local files. Deleting a chat thread does not automatically delete documents saved separately to your Desk.

You can delete your Noko account from Me → Account. Access is disabled immediately, server account data is removed within 30 days, and encrypted backups expire within a further 30 days. Deleting your Noko account does not cancel an Apple subscription or erase local files on your device; manage those separately.

Minimal pseudonymous deletion and security audit records may be retained beyond that period to prevent deleted accounts from being reactivated after a backup restore and to protect the service.

Contact and updates

For support or requests to access, correct, or delete personal information, contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or unnecessary private documents. Support messages are used solely to handle your request.

We update this page when material changes occur. These terms do not limit your statutory rights.
