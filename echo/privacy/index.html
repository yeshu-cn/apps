隐私政策与用户协议

Updated October 17, 2026 · 更新日期：2026 年 10 月 17 日

Privacy Policy and Terms

This notice applies to Echo (fun.yeshu.echo), provided by Yeshu. You retain rights to your content and must have permission to use it. AI responses can be inaccurate; check important information and keep copies of important documents.

Accounts and service data

Echo offers Sign in with Apple for its hosted model service. We process your Apple account identifier, the display name you choose to share, authentication credentials, subscription transactions and status, and model usage and credit records to operate your account, verify purchases, prevent misuse, and provide service. Apple handles payment information; Echo does not receive your card number. We do not include advertising or advertising tracking.

Your content and third parties

Chats, saved documents, personal memory, skills, and settings are stored on your device or in the workspace you select. When you use Echo's hosted model service, the relevant messages, history, retrieved documents, memory, and tool parameters pass through our server to the configured model provider. The current provider is DeepSeek. Echo does not retain chat bodies in its service database or use them for advertising. The service retains request identifiers, usage, billing and security records. Network and error logs may contain connection information.

If you configure your own model endpoint, relevant content is sent to that service. Optional remote MCP tools receive the information needed for the actions you request. Model providers and external tools may process data outside your country, under their own policies. Only connect services you trust and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Provider policies: DeepSeek · OpenRouter · OpenAI · Alibaba Cloud · Apple.

Subscriptions and your choices

Optional Echo Pro subscriptions are billed by Apple at the price and period shown before purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically unless canceled in your Apple subscription settings before renewal. You can manage or cancel subscriptions through Apple and restore eligible purchases in Echo. Service availability and usage limits are shown in the app and may depend on your plan, network, and provider availability. TestFlight purchases use Apple's test environment.

Deletion and retention

You can delete chats and saved content, disconnect external services, or delete your Echo account from the Account page. Deleting a chat does not delete separately saved documents. Account deletion disables access immediately and schedules server account data for removal within 30 days. Encrypted backups expire within a further 30 days. Deleting an account does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete device files and external copies; manage these separately. Requests about data already received by third parties should be directed to those services.

Contact

Contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com for support or privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, API keys, or unnecessary private documents. Support messages are used to handle your request. We update this page for material changes. These terms do not limit your statutory rights.

中文说明

本说明适用于 Yeshu 提供的回声（Echo，fun.yeshu.echo）。你保留内容权利，请仅处理有权使用的内容；AI 可能出错，请核对重要信息并保留备份。

账号与信息处理

使用回声官方模型服务可通过 Apple 登录。服务器处理 Apple 账号标识、你分享的显示名称、认证凭据、订阅交易与状态、模型用量及额度记录，用于提供服务、验证购买和防止滥用。Apple 处理付款，回声不接收银行卡号；应用不内置广告或广告跟踪。

聊天、成果、记忆、技能和设置保存在设备或你选择的工作区。使用官方模型时，相关输入、历史、检索资料、记忆和工具参数经回声服务器发送至配置的模型供应商，当前为 DeepSeek。服务数据库不保存聊天正文，也不将其用于广告；保留请求标识、计量、账单和安全记录，网络与错误日志可能含连接信息。自定义模型接口及远程 MCP 工具按所需接收相关内容，数据可能在境外处理，适用各服务政策。

订阅与删除

Echo Pro 为可选的 Apple 自动续订订阅，价格和周期以确认购买前显示的信息为准。可在 Apple 订阅设置中管理或取消，在回声中恢复有效购买。TestFlight 内购使用 Apple 测试环境。

你可管理和删除会话、成果或外部服务配置，并在 Account 页面删除回声账号。账号立即停用，服务端账号资料在 30 天内清除，加密备份最长再保留 30 天。删除账号不会取消 Apple 订阅，也不会删除本机文件或第三方副本，需要分别管理。删除聊天与删除独立成果也是不同操作。

支持及隐私请求请联系 guan.yeshu@gmail.com，不要发送密码或密钥。支持邮件仅用于处理请求；重要变化会更新本页，本说明不限制你的法定权利。
