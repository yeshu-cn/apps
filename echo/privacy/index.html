隐私政策与用户协议

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 17 日 · Updated October 17, 2026

中文 · English

关于本说明

本说明适用于 Yeshu 提供的回声（Echo）。你保留自己内容的权利，请仅处理有权使用的内容。AI 回答可能出错，请核对重要信息，并妥善保存重要资料。

账户与付款

使用 Apple 登录时，回声会处理 Apple 账户标识、你选择分享的名称，以及登录验证信息，用于创建和验证回声账户。回声还会处理订阅交易、订阅状态和服务使用记录，用于提供服务、验证购买和防止滥用。

付款由 Apple 处理，回声不会收到你的银行卡号。回声不含广告，也不进行广告跟踪。

你的内容如何使用

对话、保存的内容、记忆和设置保存在你的设备或你选择的资料目录中。登录账户不会自动将这些内容同步到其他设备。

使用回声的 AI 服务时，任务所需的消息、图片、相关对话、资料和记忆会经回声发送给 AI 服务商，用于生成回复、整理内容和延续记忆。当前服务商为 DeepSeek。使用前，应用会说明接收方并征求你的同意。

回声的账户记录不包含聊天正文，也不会将聊天内容用于广告。为提供服务和保障安全，回声会保留必要的服务使用、交易和安全记录；网络与错误记录可能包含连接信息。

如果你选择其他 AI 服务，相关内容会发送给你选择的服务商。使用外部工具时，工具提供方会收到完成该操作所需的信息，工具返回的内容也可能交给 AI 处理。各服务按其隐私政策处理资料，资料可能在你所在国家或地区以外处理。请只连接你信任的服务，并避免发送不必要的敏感信息。

服务商隐私政策：DeepSeek、OpenRouter、OpenAI、阿里云、Apple。

订阅与取消

Echo Pro 是可选的自动续订订阅。价格、订阅周期和权益以确认购买前显示的信息为准；除非你在续费前取消，否则订阅会自动续费。

你可以在 Apple 的订阅设置中管理或取消订阅，并在回声中恢复有效购买。服务是否可用及使用限制，以应用中的实际状态为准。

撤回授权与删除

你可以在「账户」中撤回 AI 资料授权，停止发送新的 AI 请求；撤回不影响已发送的资料。你也可以删除对话和保存的成果，或断开外部服务。删除对话不会同时删除单独保存的成果。

你可以从「账户」页面删除回声账户。账户会立即停用，服务端账户资料在 30 天内清除，加密备份最长再保留 30 天。删除账户不会取消 Apple 订阅，也不会删除本机文件或第三方保存的副本，需要分别管理。

如需处理第三方已经收到的资料，请联系相应服务提供方。

联系与更新

如需帮助，或希望查询、更正、删除个人信息，请联系 guan.yeshu@gmail.com。请勿发送密码或不必要的私人资料。支持邮件用于处理你的请求。

本说明发生重要变化时，我们会更新本页。本说明不限制你依法享有的权利。

Privacy Policy and Terms

This notice applies to Echo, provided by Yeshu. You retain rights to your content and must have permission to use it. AI responses can be inaccurate. Check important information and keep copies of important documents.

Your account and payments

When you sign in with Apple, Echo processes your Apple account identifier, the name you choose to share, and sign-in verification information to create and verify your Echo account. Echo also processes subscription transactions, subscription status, and service usage records to provide service, verify purchases, and prevent misuse.

Apple handles payments. Echo does not receive your card number. Echo has no advertising or advertising tracking.

How your content is used

Chats, saved content, memory, and settings stay on your device or in the folder you choose. Signing in does not automatically sync this content to other devices.

When you use Echo's AI service, the messages, images, relevant conversation history, documents, and memory needed for your task pass through Echo to the AI provider to generate replies, organize content, and maintain memory. The current provider is DeepSeek. The app identifies the recipient and asks for your permission before use.

Echo's account records do not contain chat bodies, and Echo does not use chat content for advertising. Echo retains necessary service usage, transaction, and security records to operate and protect the service. Network and error records may contain connection information.

If you choose another AI service, relevant content is sent to that provider. External tools receive the information needed to carry out your requested action, and their results may also be sent to the AI service. These services handle data under their own privacy policies and may process it outside your country or region. Connect only services you trust and avoid sending unnecessary sensitive information.

Provider privacy policies: DeepSeek, OpenRouter, OpenAI, Alibaba Cloud, and Apple.

Subscriptions and cancellation

Echo Pro is an optional auto-renewing subscription. The price, period, and benefits are shown before you confirm your purchase. Your subscription renews automatically unless you cancel before renewal.

You can manage or cancel subscriptions in Apple's subscription settings and restore eligible purchases in Echo. Service availability and usage limits are shown in the app.

Withdrawing permission and deletion

You can withdraw AI data-sharing permission from Account to stop new AI requests. This does not affect data already sent. You can also delete chats and saved documents, or disconnect external services. Deleting a chat does not delete separately saved documents.

You can delete your Echo account from Account. Access is disabled immediately, server account data is removed within 30 days, and encrypted backups expire within a further 30 days. Account deletion does not cancel an Apple subscription or delete device files and copies held by third parties. Manage these separately.

For requests about data already received by third parties, contact the relevant service provider.

Contact and updates

For support or requests to access, correct, or delete personal information, contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Do not send passwords or unnecessary private documents. Support messages are used to handle your request.

We update this page for material changes. These terms do not limit your statutory rights.
