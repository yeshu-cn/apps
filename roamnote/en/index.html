RoamNote

Color the places you have been.

A hand-drawn travel map with warm paper, pencil lines and room for your memories.

  • Tap a country, choose a color and mark your visit.
  • Add a date and a note for the places you want to remember.
  • Save or share your map, and keep your own journal backup.
  • Optional Pro unlocks regional maps, on-device photo location matching and HD exports with one purchase.

No account. No ads. Your travel records stay on your device.
