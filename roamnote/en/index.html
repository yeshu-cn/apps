RoamNote

Color the places you have been.
Keep the memories on your map.

A private travel map and journal, drawn in warm paper and pencil. Follow each country's real administrative levels, color a visited place, and add a short memory. Dates are optional.

Explore at your own pace

  • Tap, drill down and return through the available local levels.
  • Use county outlines with municipal polygons in the U.S., and municipality boundaries in Japan. Other countries follow their actual available hierarchy.
  • Find places by name, choose a pencil color, and keep a note. Leave the date blank when you prefer.
  • Save a map image, and export or restore your own journal backup.

Your memories stay with you

No account. No ads. Your journal stays on your device. Photo-location import asks for permission and lets you review the places before saving; it does not upload or modify your photos.

Purchasing Pro

Pro is a one-time purchase with no subscription or automatic renewal. Features and local pricing are shown in the app before purchase.

Built from real map data

Coverage and administrative names vary by country. Some countries have fewer levels, and some destinations remain points where no matching municipal polygon is available.

Coming to iPhone and iPad

The first App Store release is in preparation. The new boundary version is being validated for TestFlight.
