RoamNote Support

A little help for your travel map.

How do I mark a place?

Tap a country or use Search places. Choose a color and tap Mark visited. Tap Note to add a date or memory, then Save this page. Use the World/China controls to switch maps; drag or pinch to explore.

What is free?

Manual country and Chinese province visits, colors, notes, dates, standard map images with a watermark, and backup export/import.

What does Pro unlock?

Available regional/state and Chinese city visits, on-device photo location matching, and HD map images without a watermark. Pro is a one-time purchase, with no subscription or ads. Prices appear in your App Store currency.

How do I restore Pro?

Use the same Apple Account used for purchase, connect to the internet, then open Settings → Restore purchases. If the product is unavailable, try again after checking your connection and App Store account.

Why did photo import find nothing?

Photos must have saved location metadata and be included in your authorized selection. Open iOS Settings to adjust Photos access. Review matched places before confirming; regional boundaries may be outdated.

How do I move my journal?

Before changing devices or deleting the app, use Settings → Export backup. Save the JSON somewhere safe, then Import backup on the other device. Import adds missing entries while preserving existing records and merges local photo references. Original photos are not included and photo references may not resolve on another device.

How do I share a map?

Open Print, wait for the map preview and tap Save or share this page. Pro adds HD export without a watermark. Notes and original photo locations remain private.
