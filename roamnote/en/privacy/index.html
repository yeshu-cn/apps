Privacy Policy · Terms of Use

Privacy Policy

RoamNote is provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. This policy describes the iOS app.

Your travel journal

Places, colors, dates and notes are stored on your device. We do not operate an app account system or a server that receives these records.

Optional photo location matching

With your permission and RoamNote Pro, the app reads location metadata, capture dates and local identifiers from photos you authorize. Matching happens on your device. The app does not upload your photos, modify your library or request your live location. You may allow selected photos only and change photo access in iOS Settings. Photos without saved locations cannot be matched.

Purchases

Apple processes purchases. The app uses StoreKit and verifies transaction entitlements on your device to unlock or restore Pro. We do not receive your payment card information. Apple handles its services under its own privacy policies.

No advertising or tracking

The app contains no advertisements, advertising identifiers, analytics SDKs or cross-app tracking.

Backups, sharing and deletion

Travel records are excluded from automatic iCloud device backup. Export and keep your own backup before changing devices or uninstalling. A backup includes travel entries and local photo references, not original photo files. Map images shared from the app contain the map and visit counts, not your notes or original photo locations. Exported files are sent only to destinations you choose through iOS. Those destinations have their own privacy practices. Remove entries inside the app or uninstall to delete app records. Delete your separately exported copies where you saved them. Deleting the app does not cancel ownership of an Apple purchase.

Children

RoamNote is a general audience travel journal. We do not operate child accounts or knowingly collect children's personal data through the app.

Contact and changes

For privacy or support questions, email guan.yeshu@gmail.com. If you contact us, we use the information you choose to send to respond to your request. Please avoid sending private photos or journal backups unless necessary. We will update this policy if the app's practices change.

Terms of Use

Using RoamNote

RoamNote is a personal travel map and journal provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. Use the app lawfully and enter only content you have the right to use. Map boundaries and photo matches may be incomplete or outdated; review matches before confirming. The app does not provide navigation, emergency guidance or official boundary information.

Free features and Pro

Manual country and Chinese province visits, notes, dates, standard map exports and backups are available free. RoamNote Pro is a one-time non-consumable purchase that unlocks available regional/state and Chinese city visits, on-device photo location matching, and high-resolution watermark-free map images. There is no subscription or automatic renewal. Prices and features are shown before purchase and payment is confirmed by Apple. A lifetime purchase refers to the Pro entitlement for this app, not a promise that the app or an external platform will operate indefinitely.

Restores and refunds

Use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account used for purchase. Restoring Pro restores eligible purchase access, not deleted travel records or original photos. Refund requests are handled by Apple under its applicable rules. If a transaction is revoked or refunded, new Pro actions may become unavailable; saved records, viewing and backups remain accessible.

Your records and backups

Travel entries stay on your device and are excluded from automatic iCloud device backup. Export a backup before changing devices or uninstalling. Backups do not include original photos and local photo references may not resolve on a different device. You are responsible for copies you choose to export or share. These terms do not limit your statutory rights.
