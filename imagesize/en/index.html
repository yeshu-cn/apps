ImageSize

Keep dimensions with the photo.

Updated: 2026-09-11

ImageSize · Coming soon

Photograph a planar object on a LiDAR-capable iPhone. Select its outline or mark a distance between two points, then save the dimensions with the photo.

Capture, annotate, revisit

Align the camera with the surface, select an object and adjust annotations. Save a local record, export an annotated image to Photos or share it. The first version starts with in-app capture; calibrating imported photos is not supported.

Devices and conditions

Requires iOS 17 or later and a LiDAR-capable iPhone. Follow the alignment guide and measure points on the same plane. Reflective or transparent surfaces, occlusion and camera angle can affect results. Check critical dimensions with a physical measuring tool.

Try and unlock

A fixed sample is free. New real captures require an active subscription or lifetime unlock. US pricing is $12.99 per year or $19.99 once for lifetime access. Eligible annual subscribers receive a 7-day free trial, followed by automatic yearly renewal. Other storefronts display local prices; mainland China is CNY 68/year or CNY 98 lifetime. Apple’s confirmation screen determines the final price and trial eligibility. Existing records remain viewable, editable and exportable after a subscription expires.

Photos stay on your device

Image recognition and measurement run on the phone. Apple and RevenueCat manage purchases and restoration. See the privacy policy for details. ImageSize has not yet been released on the App Store.
