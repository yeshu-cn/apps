PHOTO DIMENSION GUIDES

A furniture measurement checklist you can print

Before checking a shelf, cabinet panel or piece of furniture, decide what the number needs to answer. “How wide is the object?” and “How much space is available?” are different measurements. Use this checklist with your measuring tool of choice.

Record three different kinds of size

Object: its overall width, height and depth, including protruding handles or feet. Space: the usable opening where it will sit. Access: doors and routes it must pass through. A product fitting the final space does not establish that it can reach it.

Make a photo record for each view

Keep front, side and detail views separate. For a flat panel, show which face and edge each dimension refers to. Capture opening and clearance details separately instead of connecting points across different depths. SnapDim can keep dimensions with a newly captured planar surface; it does not simulate whether furniture will fit or move through a doorway.

Use the checklist

Tick each item after checking it. Print the page to write your numbers in the table. The checkboxes are temporary and stay in this browser page; no entries are sent to a server.

Before ordering or cutting

Recheck the critical sizes with a physical measuring tool and use the product or installer’s required tolerances. This checklist does not calculate installation allowances, structural requirements or manufacturing tolerances.
