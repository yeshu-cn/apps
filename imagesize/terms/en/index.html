ImageSize is distributed through the App Store and uses the Apple Standard End User License Agreement. This page describes the product and purchase options without limiting your applicable consumer rights.

Measurement conditions

Real capture requires a LiDAR-capable iPhone and camera permission. Measurements depend on the detected plane, camera pose, distance, surface and selected annotations. Verify critical dimensions with physical tools. The app does not promise a specific millimeter accuracy or replace professional inspection.

Annual subscription and trial

The annual subscription unlocks full measurement and renews automatically. Eligible Apple accounts receive a 7-day free trial after confirming an annual subscription, followed by yearly billing. App Store pricing and eligibility apply. To avoid renewal, cancel in Apple subscription management at least 24 hours before the current trial or subscription ends. Uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.

Lifetime and restoration

Lifetime unlock is a one-time, non-consumable purchase for full measurement access and does not renew. It does not cancel an existing annual subscription; check Apple subscription management separately. On reinstall or another device, use the purchasing Apple account and Restore Purchases. Request refunds through Apple Report a Problem; Apple handles these requests.

Your records

The fixed sample is free. Existing records remain viewable, editable and exportable after subscription expiry. Records are stored locally; keep copies you need. Restoring purchases does not restore deleted measurement photos. A purchase does not promise compatibility with all future hardware or access to all future separate products.
