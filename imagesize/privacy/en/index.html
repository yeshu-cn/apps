Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-09-21

Scope

This policy applies to SnapDim. The app has no developer-operated accounts, advertising or cross-app advertising tracking.

Information and permissions

Photos, depth information and records you create are processed and stored on-device, not uploaded to the developer or RevenueCat. Manage camera and add-only Photos permissions in system settings. Files you share go to the services you select.

Purchase services

Apple handles payments; we do not receive payment-card details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous user identifier, purchase history, subscription status and necessary app, system-version and region information for verification, restoration and subscription analytics. It does not receive your name, email or local content. Processing may occur abroad; see the RevenueCat and Apple privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Delete local records in the app and manage external copies and system backups separately. Purchase records are kept as needed for entitlement verification, support or legal obligations. Deleting records or uninstalling does not cancel subscriptions.

Contact and updates

Support emails are used to respond and resolve issues. Contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of identifiable support information. GitHub Pages may process website access logs. We update this policy for material data-processing changes.
