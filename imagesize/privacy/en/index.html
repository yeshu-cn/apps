Privacy Policy

Updated: 2026-09-11

Scope

This policy applies to the ImageSize iOS app. Contact the developer through the support email below. The app has no developer-operated sign-in account, advertisements or cross-app advertising tracking.

Photos and measurements

Captured photos, depth information, object recognition, measurement calculations and annotations are processed and stored locally. They are not uploaded to the developer or RevenueCat. Capture requires camera permission; saving an annotated image to Photos requires add-only photo access. Manage permissions in iOS Settings. Images are passed to another app or service only when you choose to share them.

Purchases

Apple processes payments; the developer does not receive payment card details. RevenueCat validates purchases, restores access and provides subscription analytics. It processes an anonymous app user identifier, purchase history, subscription status and necessary app, system version and locale information. The app does not send your name, email, photos or measurement records to RevenueCat or include an additional behavioral analytics SDK. It does not use purchase history for cross-app advertising tracking.

Service providers

Purchase services may process related data outside your country. See the RevenueCat Privacy Policy and Apple Privacy Policy for their processing practices.

Retention and deletion

Delete local measurements from your history. Copies in Photos, shared destinations or system backups must be managed separately. Purchase records support access and restoration and may be retained for transaction verification, support or legal obligations. Deleting records or uninstalling the app does not cancel a subscription. Contact support to ask about or request deletion of personal information in support correspondence.

Support and website

Information you voluntarily email is used to reply and resolve your support request. Do not send passwords or sensitive photos. This website is hosted by GitHub Pages, which may process network request information. Visiting it does not upload your app photos. We will update this policy when data practices change.
