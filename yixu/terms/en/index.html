Subscription and Use Terms

Applicable terms

The app license uses the Apple Standard EULA linked below. This page explains Yixu Plus service and billing rules and does not limit your rights under applicable law. Updated September 17, 2026.

Monthly and yearly plans

Monthly and yearly plans auto-renew at their respective intervals and offer the same AI features and 100 credits per subscription month. Current configured mainland China prices are CNY 68/month and CNY 648/year; the final price, currency and period are shown by Apple before purchase. Annual billing is charged in full, while credits replenish monthly. No free trial is currently offered.

Credits and replenishment

Recognition, avatar creation and rendering each cost 1 credit; recommendations cost 2. Recommendation plus rendering costs 3. Credits replenish on the UTC monthly anniversary of the original subscription date, aligned to the last day for short months. Unused credits do not roll over and cannot be transferred or redeemed for cash. Rendering is limited to 20 per day, resetting at 00:00 UTC. Request-rate and service-wide capacity protections also apply. TestFlight sandbox subscriptions receive up to 10 credits per real subscription month.

Failures, restoration and refunds

Server-confirmed failures return the credits for that operation. If recommendation succeeds but rendering fails, only the rendering credit is returned. A client disconnect after server completion is not a failed operation. Restore using the original Apple purchase account and Yixu sign-in account; a purchase cannot be shared across different Yixu accounts. Monetary refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com. Credit returns are not payment refunds.

Renewal, cancellation and changes

Your Apple account is charged when you confirm a purchase. Subscriptions renew automatically. Manage, switch or cancel in Apple subscription settings; cancel at least 24 hours before renewal to avoid the next charge. After cancellation, access continues through the current valid period. Expiration or refund stops new AI calls; local saved content remains accessible. Monthly/yearly switches follow the effective date displayed by Apple. Deleting an account or uninstalling does not cancel a subscription.

AI and availability

Only upload photos you have the right to use. Generated previews do not guarantee identity, garment details, size or fit and are not professional or medical advice. AI needs internet access and may be affected by provider outages, networks or maintenance. Keep independent backups of important photos.
