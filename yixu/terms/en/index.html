Subscription and Use Terms

Applicable terms

Yixu uses Apple’s Standard EULA. These additional subscription rules do not limit your statutory rights. Updated September 21, 2026.

Subscriptions and credits

Monthly and yearly plans renew automatically, offer the same benefits and have no free trial. Yearly plans are charged in full. Both plans replenish 100 credits per subscription month, without rollover. Apple confirms pricing and currency. Costs are shown before each action: recognition, avatar creation and rendering cost 1 credit each; recommendations cost 2. Up to 20 renders daily.

Replenishment and failures

Credits replenish on the original purchase day each month, or month-end for shorter months. Daily limits reset at 00:00 UTC. Credits cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash. Confirmed failed calls return their credits; completed portions do not. A lost connection does not itself mean processing failed.

Renewal, restore and refunds

Manage or cancel in Apple subscription settings at least 24 hours before renewal. Canceled plans remain available through the current valid period; refunds or expiry end paid access, while local content remains viewable. Restore using the original Apple purchase account and Yixu sign-in account. Account deletion or uninstallation does not cancel subscriptions. Apple handles purchase refunds; returned credits are not payment refunds.
