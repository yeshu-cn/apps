Privacy Policy

Scope and contact

This policy applies to Yixu (fun.yeshu.yixu), provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. Contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Updated September 20, 2026.

Local wardrobe and photos

Selected garment photos are cut out on your device. Your wardrobe, personal reference photo, digital person, favorites and saved results are stored locally. Selecting photos does not give us access to your entire library. Uninstalling usually removes local app data; system backups and exported copies are managed separately.

Account and security

AI requires Sign in with Apple. Our server processes the Apple user identifier, authorization credentials, sessions and security state, and uses a hashed account identifier. We do not request your name or email. Network services process IP addresses for delivery, security and rate limiting.

AI data and recipients

With your consent, relevant actions send selected garment photos and attributes, a personal reference or avatar, optional height/weight, occasion, style, temperature and instructions. We use this information to identify garments, generate digital people and outfit previews, and personalize style advice and outfit recommendations based on your garments, reference photos and preferences. Firebase (Google) operates account, storage, queue and quota services; OpenRouter forwards requests to OpenAI models. Cloud services run in the United States and may process data outside your country. Personal photos are used for outfit generation, not identity authentication or facial-recognition templates. Third-party processing follows their policies; we do not promise immediate deletion of all provider logs.

Subscriptions and usage

Apple handles payments, renewals and refunds. We do not receive bank card details. We store transaction/original transaction identifiers, account tokens, product, environment, expiration and refund state, and AI usage for entitlement checks, credits, duplicate prevention and abuse prevention. Necessary transaction identifiers are sent to Apple to query subscription status.

Performance and diagnostics

To investigate generation delays and service failures, we record technical metrics such as processing stage, model, input size, duration and outcome. These custom diagnostic logs do not contain photos, the original text of your instructions, account identifiers or authorization tokens. The network services' own security and access logs follow their providers' policies.

Retention and deletion

Temporary rendering inputs on our service are deleted after the task ends. Results remain retrievable for 24 hours, then scheduled cleanup removes them. Task and daily usage records are retained for up to 30 days. Profile → Account Settings → Delete Account removes server account, session and image data and revokes Apple authorization; local wardrobe data is deleted separately. Necessary pseudonymous credit records remain until 90 days after the credit period, and purchase bindings until 90 days after subscription expiration, to prevent repeated quota claims; scheduled cleanup then removes them. Account deletion does not cancel Apple subscriptions or erase Apple purchase records. Provider security logs follow their own policies.

Support and tracking

Information you email is used for support. Do not send passwords, payment details or unnecessary personal photos. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of identifiable support information. The app has no advertising, cross-app tracking or advertising identifier access. This site uses GitHub Pages, whose host may process web access logs.
