Privacy Policy

About this policy

Yixu is provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. This policy explains how we handle your information. Contact: guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Updated September 21, 2026.

Information and purposes

We process photos, text and personal details you choose to provide, such as height, weight and preferences, to fulfill your requests and provide personalized results. We process sign-in identifiers, purchase and usage records to manage accounts and subscriptions, and necessary network, performance and error information to secure and maintain the service. Emails you send are used for support. We do not receive bank card details.

Your choices

We access only the photos you select, not your entire library. Relevant content is sent to third-party AI services only with your explicit consent. Declining does not prevent viewing local content. You can stop using online processing or manage permissions in system settings. Personal photos are not used for identity authentication or facial-recognition templates.

Third-party processing

Apple handles sign-in and payment information. Google (Firebase) provides online services and storage. OpenRouter and OpenAI process the content you consent to send. These services operate in the United States and may process data outside your region. Their privacy policies and retention rules are linked below. We do not sell personal information or engage in advertising or cross-app tracking. Our website host, GitHub Pages, may keep necessary access logs.

Retention

You manage locally saved content; system backups and exported copies are managed separately. Temporary images used in online processing are deleted after the task ends. Results remain available for retrieval for 24 hours; task and daily usage records are retained for up to 30 days. To verify entitlements and prevent repeated claims, necessary credit records remain until 90 days after the credit period, and purchase association records until 90 days after subscription expiration, then are removed by scheduled cleanup. Third-party security and access logs follow their providers’ policies.

Access and deletion

You can delete local content in the app. My → Account Settings → Delete Account removes your online account, sessions and images and revokes sign-in authorization. Necessary credit and purchase records follow the retention periods above. Account deletion does not automatically remove local copies, cancel Apple subscriptions or erase Apple purchase records. Contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information we can associate with you.

Security and updates

We use reasonable safeguards and restrict unnecessary access to information. Do not send passwords or payment credentials to support. We will update this policy when processing changes materially and seek consent again when needed.
