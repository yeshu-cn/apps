Yixu · Style the clothes you own

Your wardrobe, more combinations

Build a local wardrobe from selected clothing photos. Create a digital person from your reference photo, choose garments yourself or ask AI, and generate outfit previews. Yixu is currently in internal testing and is not yet publicly released on the App Store.

Yixu Plus

AI garment recognition, avatar creation, recommendations and rendering require Sign in with Apple and a verified subscription. Monthly and yearly plans provide the same benefits. Apple displays the local purchase price. Your existing local wardrobe and saved images remain available.

Usage you can see

Both plans replenish 100 credits each subscription month, without rollover. Recognition, avatar generation and rendering cost 1 credit each; recommendations cost 2. Recommendation plus rendering costs 3. Up to 20 outfit renders per day.

Your choice before upload

AI data recipients are explained before your consent. Browsing your local wardrobe does not upload photos. Generated images are visual references and do not guarantee identity, garment details, size or fit.
