Little Lines

Help & Support

Start creating

Pick a little person in Studio, or tap Create. Use Pose, Face, Props, Motion, Text and Canvas to customize your drawing. Tap Save and find it again in My Drawings.

Make WeChat stickers

My Drawings → Make WeChat Stickers → choose your drawings or built-in art → Export. Select avatars, chat icons or stickers, choose PNG or GIF, and save to Photos. The app does not automatically install stickers into WeChat. Use WeChat’s own image or sticker picker to add the exported files.

Export and Photos access

You can also export one drawing using the share icon in the editor, then share it or save to Files. If saving to Photos fails, check add-only Photos permission in iOS Settings and available device storage, then retry.

Language and version

English and Simplified Chinese follow your system’s app language setting. Open About from the top right of My Drawings to see the version, privacy policy and font licenses. Feedback opens the App Store, which may be unavailable before the first public release.

Backup and feedback

Drawings are stored on your device. Export before uninstalling. There is no account or cloud recovery service. Use About → Feedback for App Store reviews. For technical help, email guan.yeshu@gmail.com with the app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue.
