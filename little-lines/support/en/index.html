Little Lines

Help & Support

Start creating

Pick a little person in Studio, or tap Create. Use Pose, Face, Props, Motion, Text and Canvas to customize your drawing. Tap Save and find it again in My Drawings.

Make WeChat stickers

My Drawings → Make WeChat Stickers → choose your drawings or built-in art → Export. Select avatars, chat icons or stickers, choose PNG or GIF, and save to Photos. The app does not automatically install stickers into WeChat. Use WeChat’s own image or sticker picker to add the exported files.

Export and Photos access

Export one drawing using the export icon in the editor: choose a 1024 × 1024 PNG or a 512 × 512 GIF, then share it, save to Photos or save to Files. PNGs can have transparent backgrounds; GIFs use the selected canvas background. Set an effect under Motion to preview an animation before exporting. If saving to Photos fails, check add-only Photos permission in iOS Settings and available device storage, then retry.

Language and version

The app supports nine languages: English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German and Brazilian Portuguese. It follows the system by default. To choose a language, open My Drawings → About → Language; your choice is saved for next time. About also includes the version, Quick Tour and Privacy & Terms. Your saved titles and captions keep their original text when you switch languages. Feedback opens the App Store, which may be unavailable before the first public release.

Backup and feedback

Drawings are stored on your device. Export before uninstalling. There is no account or cloud recovery service. Use About → Feedback for App Store reviews. For technical help, email guan.yeshu@gmail.com with the app version, iOS version and steps to reproduce the issue.
