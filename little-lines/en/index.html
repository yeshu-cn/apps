Little Lines

Little Lines: Sticker Maker

Make every little person your own

Start with 52 built-in poses and scenes. Drag joints, pick an expression and props, and add your own words. Six line styles give your drawings a personal touch.

Turn a little feeling into a GIF

Add a wave, sway, bounce, breath or blink. Export individual drawings as 1024 × 1024 PNGs with an optional transparent background, or 512 × 512 looping GIFs with the selected canvas background. Save to Photos, share a file or save to Files.

Make a matching sticker set

Open My Drawings → Make WeChat Stickers. Choose your drawings or built-in art, combine up to 24, then export PNG images, looping GIFs, avatars and chat icons. Save the files to Photos and add or use them in WeChat yourself.

Your creativity stays on your device

No ads, accounts, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Create and export offline, and keep editable drawings on your device. Choose English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German or Brazilian Portuguese in My Drawings → About → Language.

Version and availability

For iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Version 1.0.0 is being prepared for its first App Store release. A download link will appear here when the store page becomes public.
