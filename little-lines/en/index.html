Little Lines: Stick Figures

A little character, your own words

No drawing skills required. Start with one of 52 poses and scenes, drag the joints, change the expression and add your own words. Choose from 14 expressions, 16 props and six line styles to make the character yours.

Turn a little feeling into a GIF

Add a wave, sway, bounce, breath or blink. Export individual drawings as 1024 × 1024 PNGs with an optional transparent background, or 512 × 512 looping GIFs with the selected canvas background. Save to Photos, share a file or save to Files.

Take your creations into your chats

With the app in English, open My Drawings → Make WhatsApp Stickers. Combine 3 to 30 drawings into a static or animated sticker pack. Tap Add to WhatsApp to review and confirm the pack in the installed WhatsApp app, or share the sticker files. Other app languages offer WeChat PNG/GIF images, with an optional cover and panel icon, for you to add or use manually.

Your creativity stays on your device

No ads, accounts, subscriptions or in-app purchases. Create and export offline, and keep editable drawings on your device. Choose English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese, Japanese, Korean, Spanish, French, German or Brazilian Portuguese in My Drawings → About → Language.

Version and launch offer

For iPhone with iOS 17 or later. Version 1.0.0 is being prepared for App Store review. The launch plan is free download for the first two weeks after public release, followed by a one-time download price. This is a download promotion, not a trial that locks the app after 14 days. The App Store will show the price available at the time of download. A download link will appear here when the store page is public.
