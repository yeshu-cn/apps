Little Lines

Privacy Policy

Scope

This policy applies to Little Lines / 一笔小人 (fun.yeshu.LittleLines), provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. Updated September 21, 2026.

Local creation and data

Poses, expressions, captions, styles and saved drawings remain in the app’s local storage. Drawing and PNG/GIF rendering happen on your device. The app has no accounts, uploads to developer servers, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics SDKs. We do not collect data through this app.

Photos permission

The app requests add-only Photos access when you choose to save exported images or GIFs. It does not read your photo library. You can decline and still create drawings and use system sharing or file export. You can change access in iOS Settings.

Sharing and external pages

System sharing sends only your selected files to the app or service you choose, when you initiate it. Feedback opens the App Store; policy and help links open a website. Those services handle information under their own policies. The website hosting provider may process access logs.

Storage, backup and deletion

Long-press a drawing in My Drawings to delete it. Uninstalling removes the app’s local data. System backups, exported files and Photos copies are managed separately by you and the operating system; deleting an in-app drawing does not delete these copies. Export what you need before uninstalling.

Support and contact

If you email us, we use the information you provide to reply and resolve the issue. Do not send passwords or unnecessary personal information. For privacy questions or deletion of identifiable support correspondence, contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com.
