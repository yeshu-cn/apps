Privacy Policy

Scope

This policy applies to Little Lines, provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. Updated September 21, 2026.

Information and permissions

Content you create is processed and saved locally, not uploaded to the developer. The app has no accounts, advertising, tracking or third-party analytics and does not collect user data through the app. Add-only Photos access is requested when you choose to save; the app does not read your library. Declining does not prevent local use. Manage permissions in system settings.

Sharing and third parties

Only files you choose to share are passed to your selected service. The App Store, external websites and website hosts process necessary information, including access logs, under their own policies.

Storage and deletion

Delete content in the app. Uninstalling removes local data; manage exported files, Photos copies and system backups separately. Keep backups of important content.

Contact and updates

Support emails are used only to respond and resolve issues. Contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of identifiable support information. We update this policy for material changes and seek consent when needed.
