隐私政策与用户协议

更新日期：2026 年 10 月 28 日

中文 · English

适用范围

本说明适用于成都荣光科技有限公司（Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd.）提供的承谱。你保留所记录内容的权利，请仅使用有权记录、保存和分享的资料。

资料与权限

承谱无需注册账号，不含广告、跟踪或第三方统计。成员姓名、亲属关系、生卒年份、籍贯、文字、照片与家族记事保存在设备上，不会上传至我们的服务器，也不会供我们远程查看。

添加照片时使用 Apple 系统照片选择器，仅处理你选中的照片，不读取整个相册。导入备份时仅读取你选定的文件。承谱不申请通讯录、位置、麦克风或相机权限。

备份与分享

系统设备备份是否包含应用资料，由你的 Apple 账户与系统设置决定，由 Apple 按其政策处理。承谱不提供自己的云同步服务。

导出备份、PDF 或 CSV 后，文件会交给你选择的系统存储位置、应用或接收人。完整备份是可读取的文件，未额外设置密码；请妥善保管，避免公开传播。在世成员的资料与照片应取得本人同意；涉及未成年人时，应征得监护人同意。

保留与删除

资料保留到你主动删除。可删除单个成员、记事和照片，或在族谱设置中清除本机全部资料及自动恢复副本。删除成员会解除其关系，记事正文仍会保留。卸载应用通常会移除本机应用资料；系统备份、你导出的文件与他人保存的副本需分别管理。

在替换族谱或恢复备份前，应用会保留一份替换前的恢复副本；再次替换会更新这份副本。请另行备份重要资料。

使用约定

承谱用于个人与家庭资料整理。亲属称谓与关系路径是整理参考，不构成亲属身份、继承权或其他法律关系的证明。不确定的资料可以留空或标注来源；请尊重家人隐私与不同家庭的实际情况。当前版本无付费功能。

联系与更新

如需帮助或提出个人信息请求，请联系 guan.yeshu@gmail.com。主动发送给我们的支持邮件仅用于处理你的请求，请勿附上无关的家人资料或完整族谱。我们无法远程恢复你未备份的本机资料。

访问支持网站时，网站托管方可能处理提供网页所需的常规网络连接信息；Apple 服务适用其隐私政策。本说明发生重要变化时会更新本页，不限制你依法享有的权利。

Privacy Policy and Terms

ChengPu is provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. You retain rights to your content and must have permission to record, store, and share it.

Your data

No account is required. ChengPu has no advertising, tracking, third-party analytics, or in-app purchases. Names, family relationships, years, places, biographies, photographs, and stories stay on your device. They are not uploaded to our servers or available for us to inspect remotely.

The Apple photo picker provides only the photos you select. Import reads only your chosen file. The app does not request contacts, location, microphone, or camera access.

Backups and sharing

Apple device backups depend on your system and account settings and are handled by Apple. ChengPu does not operate a cloud sync service. When you export or share a backup, PDF, or CSV, the file is handed to the location, app, or recipient you choose. Backup files are readable and are not separately password protected. Keep them private. Obtain living relatives' consent, or guardian consent for children, before recording and sharing their information.

Retention and deletion

Data remains until you delete it. Delete individual people, stories, or photos, or clear all local data and the automatic recovery copy in settings. Removing a person removes their links; story text remains. Uninstalling normally removes local app data. Manage device backups, exported files, and recipients' copies separately.

Replacing or restoring a family book retains one recovery copy of the previous book. A later replacement updates that copy. Keep independent backups of important content.

Use and contact

ChengPu is for personal family record keeping. Relationship names are references, not evidence of legal kinship, inheritance, or other legal status. Leave unknown facts blank or record their sources, and respect family members' privacy.

For support or privacy requests, email guan.yeshu@gmail.com. Voluntary support messages are used to handle your request. Do not send unrelated family information or an entire genealogy. We cannot remotely recover local data you have not backed up.

The support website's hosting provider may process ordinary connection information needed to serve pages. Apple services follow Apple's Privacy Policy. Material changes will be posted here. These terms do not limit statutory rights.
