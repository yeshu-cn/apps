Privacy policy

Updated September 21, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to AquaTrue. The app has no accounts, advertising, behavioral analytics or cross-app tracking.

Information and permissions

Selected videos, associated camera information and settings are processed on your device and are not sent to the developer. Manage Photos access in system settings. Apple downloads originals stored in iCloud, which may use network data.

Purchases and third parties

Apple processes purchases and restoration; we do not receive payment-card details. Files you choose to share go to the services you select under their policies. GitHub Pages, our website host, may process access logs.

Storage and deletion

You manage local content. Save temporary files yourself; the in-app list is not a permanent backup. Uninstalling removes local data. Delete copies in Photos, Files, shared destinations and system backups separately. Local deletion does not erase Apple purchase records.

Contact and updates

Support emails are used to respond and resolve issues. Contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com to request access, correction or deletion of identifiable support information, except where retention is legally required. Do not send passwords or sensitive content. We update this policy for material changes and seek consent when needed.
