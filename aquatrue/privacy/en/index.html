AquaTrue

Privacy policy

Updated September 7, 2026

Scope

This policy applies to AquaTrue 1.0.0. Video import, color analysis, preview, restoration, and export run on your device. The current app has no developer-operated account system or video upload server, advertisements, or third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.

Photos and videos

The app processes videos you select and available camera metadata to preview, restore colors, and tune camera profiles. These are not sent to the developer. Manage Photos access in system settings. If an original is stored in iCloud, Apple Photos downloads it, which may use network data.

Purchases

Apple and StoreKit handle payments and restoration. The app verifies purchase entitlements on-device. The developer does not receive your payment card details. Apple's privacy policy and service terms govern its transaction processing.

Local storage and deletion

Settings, custom presets, and imported copies stay on-device. Exports create temporary files; the project list is not a permanent library. Save finished videos using system sharing. Uninstalling removes local app data; system backups depend on your settings. Delete exported copies and backups separately in Photos, Files, or the service where you saved them. Removing app data does not remove App Store purchase records.

Sharing and support

A video is handed to another app or service only when you choose a sharing destination. Information you voluntarily email is used to respond and investigate the issue. Your email provider handles transmission. Do not send sensitive videos or credentials. You may email us to request deletion of personal information in support correspondence, except where retention is legally required.

Tracking and changes

The app does not track you across apps or read advertising identifiers. If data practices change, this policy will be updated and authorization requested where applicable. This policy concerns the app. GitHub Pages hosts this support website and may process network request information; visiting it does not upload your app videos.

Contact

Email guan.yeshu@gmail.com with your app version, device model, and a description of the issue.
