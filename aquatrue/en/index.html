AquaTrue

AquaTrue

Updated September 7, 2026

Bring underwater colors back to life

A simple underwater video color restoration tool. Choose Natural, Clear, or Vivid, adjust strength, compare with a split view, then export. English and Simplified Chinese are supported. The first release is in preparation and is not yet available on the App Store.

Simple controls, finer adjustments

Expand Advanced to fine-tune red recovery, green correction, water tone, contrast, saturation, and brightness. Save your own presets.

Camera-aware tuning

Available metadata can help identify selected GoPro, DJI Action, Insta360, and iPhone footage. You can override the camera profile. Detection is not guaranteed; results depend on the footage and water conditions.

Try before you buy

Import, adjustments, and previews are free. A non-consumable lifetime purchase unlocks unlimited watermark-free exports. No subscription. The purchase confirmation shows the current local price.
