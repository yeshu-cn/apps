Guide Ledger

Terms of use

September 8, 2026

Purpose

Guide Ledger is developed by YESHU to record trip income and expenses, show statistics and generate reports. Results depend on the records you enter. Check amounts, categories and original receipts before submitting a report.

Your records

You retain your rights in the content you enter. Make sure you have permission to record and share information concerning others. You decide who receives exported files.

Backups and imports

The current version stores records locally on your device. Export a backup before changing devices, uninstalling or importing another backup. Importing replaces existing data. Keep exported backups secure.

Membership and billing

Basic bookkeeping, including creating and editing trips and ledger entries, is free. Weekly, annual and lifetime plans unlock income statistics, trends and report exports. Weekly and annual plans renew automatically unless cancelled in your store. Lifetime is a non-consumable one-time purchase for these features with no recurring fee. Available plans, local prices and payment terms are shown by the store at purchase. Cancelling renewal normally preserves access until the paid period ends; access follows RevenueCat entitlement status. After expiry, bookkeeping and JSON backup/import remain available; statistics and report exports require active membership.

Restore, manage and refund purchases

Use Restore purchases on the Membership screen with the original store account. No Guide Ledger login is required; purchases are restored within the original store, and cross-store transfer is not currently supported. Use Manage subscription to open your store settings. Weekly and annual changes are managed in the store; buying lifetime does not automatically cancel a separate subscription. Refund requests are handled through the store where the purchase was made. Uninstalling the app does not cancel an auto-renewing subscription.

Apps obtained through the App Store are also subject to Apple’s standard End User License Agreement unless the store states otherwise. These usage notes do not limit your statutory consumer rights.
