Guide Ledger

Privacy policy

September 6, 2026

Scope

This policy describes how Guide Ledger 1.1.0, developed by YESHU, handles data. It applies to Guide Ledger, not to SnowCut or any file storage or sharing service you choose.

Information you enter

The app stores agency details, trips, transactions, payment methods and notes on your device, along with any name, phone number, gender and avatar you choose to enter. This information is used to display your ledger, calculate statistics and produce reports. No account or sign-in is required.

Uploads and tracking

The current version has no developer-operated account server or cloud synchronization. It does not automatically upload your ledger or profile to the developer, and does not include advertising or third-party behavioral analytics. The developer cannot remotely access your ledger.

Photos and files

When you choose an avatar, the app uses the system photo picker and saves the selected image in its local directory. Backup import processes only the file you select. You can manage permissions in system settings. An avatar is optional.

Exporting and sharing

When you choose to export or share, the app creates a PDF or JSON file and passes it to the system sharing interface. These files may contain trip, financial and profile information. You choose the destination app or storage service, whose rules and your settings then govern transfer, retention and deletion. Review the content and recipient before sharing.

Retention and deletion

Local records remain until you delete them in the app or remove its local data. Uninstalling the app may remove local records, so make a backup first if needed. Exported files are not deleted when records are removed from the app; delete those files separately from their storage locations.

Device backups

Your operating system may back up app data according to your device settings. Such backups are managed by the system or backup provider, not uploaded to the developer by Guide Ledger. Manage or delete them through the relevant service.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds accounts, synchronization or other data handling, this policy will be updated and consent requested where needed. Instructions for current data operations are available in Help.

Contact

Email guan.android2011@gmail.com with the app version, device model and a description of the issue. Do not send real ledgers, identity documents or other sensitive information. Redact personal details from screenshots. Your chosen email service transmits the message; information you voluntarily send is used to respond to your question or investigate the issue.
