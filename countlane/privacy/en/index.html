Privacy Policy & Terms of Use

For Countlane. Last updated: October 17, 2026.

Service and use

Countlane is provided by Chengdu Rong Glory Technology Co., Ltd. for personal counting and tabletop scoring. Use the software lawfully and respect others’ rights. You retain rights to content you enter and export; the software, branding, and built-in assets belong to their respective rights holders.

Content and permissions

Counter names, values, targets, history, selected or captured photos, photo marks, player names, and scores are processed and saved on the device and are not sent to the developer. No app account is required. The app includes no ads, tracking, or third-party analytics. Camera access is requested only when you choose to take a photo. The system photo picker provides photos you select without requiring access to your entire library. The app does not request contacts, location, or microphone permission.

Optional iCloud and sharing

If you enable iCloud sync for counter sets, their records are stored in your Apple Account’s private iCloud storage and handled by Apple under its service rules. Photo counts and tabletop scores stay on this device and are outside that sync and counter-set library backups. Only when you initiate sharing or export is the selected result, image, or file passed to your chosen service, which handles its copy under its own rules.

Purchases

Optional Pro in-app purchases and purchase restoration are handled by Apple. The developer does not receive your payment-card or payment-account details. Purchases and refunds are subject to Apple’s store terms and applicable law.

Retention and deletion

You can delete counter sets; when sync is enabled, deletions also sync to connected devices. A new photo or table replaces the corresponding current task. Uninstalling normally removes local app data. Records in iCloud, exports, recipient copies, and system backups must be managed separately. The developer does not offer hosted data storage or recovery. Keep copies of important results.

Contact, website, and updates

Support emails are used only to handle your request. Send only the information needed to resolve your issue. Contact guan.yeshu@gmail.com to request access to, correction of, or deletion of information you provide through support. The website host may keep necessary access logs.
