Countlane

Quick counts. Shared scoreboards.

Count what is in front of you

Use Countlane for everyday counting or a game around the table. Create a photo count, tabletop scoreboard, or quick counter project from the home screen. Existing projects stay available so you can return and continue.

Mark a photo, one item at a time

Choose or take a photo, then tap each item to place a numbered mark. Pinch to zoom, pan, remove a mistaken mark, or undo. Each photo and its marks save automatically as an independent project. Save an image with the marks and total to Photos, or share it. Counting is manual, without automatic object recognition.

Keep the whole table in view

Keep scores for 2–12 players or teams. Tap a score, use the plus and minus buttons, or enter a positive or negative score change. Undo mistakes. Table and player names save automatically, and you can reopen the same table from the home screen.

Simple saving, clear boundaries

No app account, ads, or third-party analytics. Projects save automatically on your device and can be renamed, pinned, or archived. Quick counters and tabletop scores offer optional iCloud sync and counter-library backups. Photo projects are outside that sync and backup; keep a separate copy of the marked image.
