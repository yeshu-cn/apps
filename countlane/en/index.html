Countlane

Quick counts. Shared scoreboards.

Count what is in front of you

Use Countlane for a quick count or a game around the table. Open Photo count or Tabletop scoring from the home screen, or keep using familiar quick counters.

Mark a photo, one item at a time

Choose or take a photo, then tap each item to place a numbered mark. The total follows your marks. Pinch to zoom, pan, remove a mistaken mark, or undo, then share an image with the marks and total. Counting is manual, without automatic object recognition.

Keep the whole table in view

Start a table for 2–12 players or teams. Add and subtract board-game points, transfer agreed points from several players to one recipient in a single action, or keep separate round scores and totals for team games. Confirm a round to start the next, and correct completed rounds when needed.

Simple saving, clear boundaries

No app account, ads, or third-party analytics. Your current photo and table stay on this device and resume when you return. A new task replaces the current one, so share results you want to keep first. Counter sets offer optional iCloud sync and library backups; photo counts and tabletop scores are outside both.
