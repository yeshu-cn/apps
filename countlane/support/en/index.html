Help & Support

Countlane

Photo count

Create a Photo count project from the home screen. Take or choose a photo, then tap each item to add a numbered mark. Pinch to zoom and pan, select a mistaken mark to remove it, or use undo and redo. The photo and marks save automatically; reopen the project from the home screen to continue editing. Save the image with its marks and total to Photos, or share it. This is manual counting, without automatic object recognition.

Camera and photo access

Camera permission is needed only when you choose to take a photo. If you decline, you can still import selected photos through the system photo picker, or allow the camera later in Settings. Save to Photos requests permission only to add photos. If you decline, you can continue counting or use sharing. The app does not request permission to read your entire photo library.

Tabletop scoring

Create a Tabletop scoring project from the home screen for 2–12 players or teams. Tap a score or use the plus and minus buttons. Use Record score to enter a positive or negative change and preview the new score before saving. Undo mistakes. You can rename the table and players; scores and changes save automatically.

Projects and keeping results

Photo counts, tabletop scores, and quick counters are independent projects. Creating a project keeps your existing projects. Rename, pin, archive, or delete them from the home screen. Archived projects remain readable; restore one to continue editing. Photo projects stay on this device and are outside iCloud sync and counter-library backups. Save a marked image to Photos or share it to keep a separate image copy.

Quick counters and backups

Tap or swipe up to increase a quick counter, swipe down to decrease it, or use the plus and minus buttons. Quick counter sets support steps, targets, and history. In Settings, iCloud sync can sync quick counters and tabletop scores. Backup & restore can export a counter-library backup and restore independent copies while keeping existing records. Backups include archived and pinned sets, settings, and undo records; photo projects are excluded. Sync propagates changes and deletions, so keep important backups separately.

Purchases and restoration

Pro is an optional in-app purchase. Check the purchase screen for the purchase type and price. To restore, use the original purchasing Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases. A purchase being unavailable does not delete existing records. For billing or refunds, use Apple’s purchase history and official refund channels.

Contact support

Email guan.yeshu@gmail.com with the app version, iOS/iPadOS version, steps to reproduce, and any error message. Do not send Apple Account passwords, payment details, or unnecessary private photos and counter content.
