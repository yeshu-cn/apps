Help & Support

Countlane

Photo count

Open Photo count from the home screen. Take or choose a photo, then tap each item to add a numbered mark. Pinch to zoom and pan, select a mistaken mark to remove it, or use undo and redo. Share the image with its marks and total when finished. This is manual counting, without automatic object recognition.

Camera and photo access

Camera permission is needed only when you choose to take a photo. If you decline, you can still import selected photos through the system photo picker, or allow the camera later in Settings. The app does not request access to your entire photo library.

Tabletop scoring

Open Tabletop scoring from the home screen for 2–12 players or teams. Free scoring supports board-game points. Point transfers update a recipient and contributing players together, for agreed scores in games such as Mahjong; the app does not calculate Mahjong hands. Round scoring keeps current-round scores separate from totals. Confirm to begin the next round, or correct a completed round to recalculate totals. Undo is available for mistakes.

Current tasks and keeping results

The app keeps one current photo and one current table so you can return after closing it. A new photo or table replaces the corresponding task. Share any result you want to keep first. Photo counts and tabletop scores stay on this device and are outside counter-set iCloud sync and library backups.

Quick counters and backups

Tap or swipe up to increase a quick counter, swipe down to decrease it, or use the plus and minus buttons. Counter sets include steps, targets, history, and CSV export. In Settings, iCloud sync can sync counter sets, and Backup & restore can export or import a counter-set library backup. CSV is for reading records, not restoring app state. Sync propagates changes and deletions; keep important backups separately.

Purchases and restoration

Pro is an optional in-app purchase. Check the purchase screen for the purchase type and price. To restore, use the original purchasing Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases. A purchase being unavailable does not delete existing records. For billing or refunds, use Apple’s purchase history and official refund channels.

Contact support

Email guan.yeshu@gmail.com with the app version, iOS/iPadOS version, steps to reproduce, and any error message. Do not send Apple Account passwords, payment details, or unnecessary private photos and counter content.
